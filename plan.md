# Video plan

## Cast

- **Main_character.PNG** (Narrator) — The main character who explains the domestication story and guides the viewer through the video
- **Fanged_guy.PNG** (Wild Wolf) — Represents the wild wolf ancestor before domestication, appears in prehistoric flashback scenes
- **Rescue_Dog_dog.png** (Domesticated Dog) — Represents the modern domesticated dog, shown in rescue/modern and natural bonding scenarios

## Background scenes

- **White Void** — Plain white background for talking heads and explanatory scenes with the narrator
- **Rubble/Disaster** — Stark pale grey with chunky broken concrete blocks for earthquake wreckage and rescue scenarios
- **Ancient/Evolutionary** — Solid tan background for prehistoric settings, early human campfire scenes, and evolutionary history
- **Outdoor/Night Fire** — Solid orange background for the original wolf-meets-human moment by firelight
- **Science/Biology** — Solid blue background for scent anatomy, brain diagrams, and oxytocin/hormonal explanation scenes
- **Outdoor/Daylight** — Flat green ground with blue sky for animal behavior, nature scenes, and modern dog-human bonding
