### CONTENT & SCRIPT DNA
- **Niche:** Human history, evolution, anthropology, psychology
- **Format:** 10–14 minute educational explainer narrated in calm 2nd-person ("you", "your brain", "your ancestors") — never "we" or "I"
- **Hook formula:** Opens with a relatable modern moment → immediately says "but the real answer is far stranger" → reframes everything
- **Script rhythm:** Short sentence. Short sentence. One longer sentence that builds depth. Short sentence. Question?
- **Narrative arc:** Hook → Reframe → Historical/Scientific Deep Dive → Counterintuitive Twist → Modern Mirror → Closing line that echoes the opening
- **No jargon without plain-English explanation** — every scientific term gets decoded immediately
- **Always ends** by reflecting the ancient or scientific truth back onto something the viewer feels or does today

### DIFFERENTIATION ENGINE
This channel does NOT invent topics from scratch. It takes a topic that already has proven search demand and attaches an angle nobody else is running. Picking a topic is step one; the video lives or dies on the angle.

For any candidate topic, first identify THE SATURATED ANGLE — the framing existing popular videos already use (this is the version to avoid). Then apply ONE differentiation operation to find the fresh angle:

1. INVERT THE VERDICT — the popular videos celebrate or condemn it; argue the defensible opposite.
2. WHAT WE GOT WRONG — the famous videos rest on a finding that was later revised, failed replication, or was widely misread. The video is the correction.
3. COLLISION — fuse two known topics nobody connects. The video is the link between them.
4. SHIFT THE PROTAGONIST — retell the famous explanation from a different subject, species, culture, or timeframe than the one everyone uses.
5. THE ONE WEIRD MECHANISM — drop the broad "why do we ___" wonder and go deep on a single strange specific sub-fact the big videos skip over entirely.

HARD BAN — these framings are burned out. Do not pitch the canonical version of: fear of the dark, why we cry, tickling yourself, Dunbar's 150, the marshmallow test as willpower, phone addiction as dopamine, "your brain is still in the savanna." A topic from this list is ONLY allowed if the chosen angle is operation 1 or 2 — actively contradicting the famous video.
